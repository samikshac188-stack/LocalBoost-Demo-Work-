A passion for the craft

Meera began her journey in beauty with a simple love for transforming how people feel about themselves. After years of training in hair and bridal artistry, she opened Aurum Noir to create the kind of salon she always wished existed.

Her vision is a calm, private studio where every guest is heard, every detail is considered and every visit ends with confidence.
